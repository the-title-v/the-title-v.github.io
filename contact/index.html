LET’S TALK ABOUT YOUR STAY

A hello is a good start.

A question about the apartments? A longer stay in mind? Send our booking team a message and start planning your time in Rawai.

Message the booking team on WhatsApp

Our direct contact number is +7 (913) 909-8949. The button opens a WhatsApp chat; you choose whether to send a message.

For a booking request, use the calendar and calculator first. It prepares a message with your chosen apartment, dates, adults and children, stay estimate, refundable deposit and utility rates.

A few useful details

Include your dates, number of adults and children, children’s ages, preferred apartment and any important requirements. Let us know if your dates are flexible or you would like early check-in or late check-out.

Our local agent meets and greets you at arrival. Share your travel timing so the handover can be arranged. Please wait for written confirmation of availability, the final price and terms before making payment.
