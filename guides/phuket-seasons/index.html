Phuket through the seasons

Drier months, tropical showers and changing seas. Choose the time of year that suits your kind of holiday.

Choose your month

Phuket stays warm all year. December to March usually makes beach and boat plans easier; May to October suits a more flexible holiday. November is a changeover month, so don’t count on settled weather just yet.

MonthsWhat to expect
January–FebruaryUsually the driest months. A popular time for beach holidays.
March–AprilHotter days, with showers becoming more frequent.
May–AugustMore rain, with showers and dry breaks.
September–OctoberTypically the wettest stretch. Keep plans flexible.
NovemberRain generally eases, but the weather is still changeable.
DecemberDrier weather becomes more established. Book holiday travel early.

Rain doesn’t always take the whole day

Some days bring a heavy shower followed by a dry stretch; others stay wet for longer. Keep a café, massage or museum visit in reserve, and use dry gaps for a walk.

The weeks around the start and end of the main season can be quieter. They work well if you’re happy to choose outings as the weather unfolds.

  • Ideas for a rainy day

Let the sea decide

West-coast beaches can have large waves and strong currents, especially from May to October. Check the flags even when the sun comes out. A red flag means stay out of the water; follow the lifeguards’ advice.

For a boat trip, ask the operator about conditions and leave a spare day in case the weather changes.

Pack for sun and showers

Bring light clothes, a hat, sunscreen and shoes with grip. A small umbrella and a waterproof phone pouch are handy. Keep water with you, and save longer walks for the cooler part of the day.
