Family time · 2 min read

Rawai with children

Easy meals, pool time and small outings make a more relaxed family holiday.

Start with sleeping and meals

Each apartment sleeps up to two adults and two children aged 14 or under, with a queen bed and a sofa bed. Tell us the children’s ages when you book and ask ahead if you need baby equipment.

The kitchen is useful for familiar breakfasts and snacks, and the washer helps with beach clothes. Tops Rawayana is an easy first stop for supplies.

Pool mornings and a beach afternoon

The residence has shared pools, a children’s pool and an outdoor playground. Stay with children around the water and play areas.

For a beach trip, try Nai Harn when the sea is calm. Bring shade and water, follow the flags, and leave time to return for a rest. One outing a day is often plenty in the heat.

  • Nai Harn & Ya Nui

A few plans beyond the pool

Rawai Park has water play, a kids club, playgrounds and a trampoline zone. It’s a useful local change of scene; take swimwear, a towel and socks.

For a longer stay, contact local nurseries and schools before arrival. Start with your child’s age, language and dates, then arrange a visit.
