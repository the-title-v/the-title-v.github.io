Phuket Airport to Rawai: taxi or bus?

A direct car for an easy arrival, or the ฿100 coastal bus. Here are the fares, pickup points and departures.

Taxi: straight to the apartment

Phuket Airport Limousine lists Rawai at ฿1,000 for a taxi with up to three passengers, or ฿1,400 for a Fortuner with up to four. Its Fortuner service is listed for 07:00–18:00. Agree the fare and luggage space when booking; request child seats ahead.

The operator’s counters are outside Domestic Exit 1 and International Exit 2. A prebooked car is the simplest option with children, several bags or a late arrival. Grab also shows a fare and pickup instructions in the app.

  • Airport Limousine prices & pickup points

Smart Bus: ฿100 along the coast

The Phuket Smart Bus runs from the Domestic Terminal through Surin, Kamala, Patong, Karon and Kata to Rawai Beach. The fare is ฿100 per person. The published journey takes about 2 hours 10 minutes to 2 hours 40 minutes, before traffic delays.

Departures below are from the operator’s timetable checked on 23 October 2026. Open the live tracker before walking to the stop.

The other airport bus goes to town

Airport Bus 8411 costs ฿100 to Phuket Town’s Bus Terminal 1. It does not continue to Rawai, so you’ll need another ride from town. Choose the Smart Bus for a direct coastal route to Rawai.

  • Airport Bus 8411 timetable
  • Airport Bus 8411 fares

Arriving at The Title V

Give your driver the full destination: The Title V (Rawai–Phuket), 555 Wiset Road. Our local agent will meet you for the handover. Send your flight details and keep the arrival instructions on your phone.

Check-in starts at 15:00. For the return trip, leave plenty of time for traffic as well as your airline’s check-in deadline.
