9 things to pack for a self-catering stay in Rawai

A stay with your own kitchen and washing machine calls for a different suitcase from a few nights in a hotel. Bright and Cozy have space for everyday routines, so pack the things that make those routines yours. Start with these nine useful choices, then check any must-have equipment with our booking team.

Updated 4 November 2026

Check dates See Bright & Cozy

1. A small rotation of washable clothes

Pack clothes you can mix, wear again and wash during your stay, plus a light bag for used laundry. Each apartment has a washing machine, which helps you avoid bringing a fresh outfit for every day of a month.

Ask us about detergent and drying arrangements before travelling so you can choose clothes that suit the setup.

2. A folding shopping bag and first-shop list

A reusable bag is useful for bringing breakfast ingredients and small grocery shops back to your own kitchen. Save a short list on your phone: drinking water, fruit, breakfast basics and ingredients for one easy meal.

Both kitchens have an electric hob, refrigerator, microwave, kettle and toaster. Plan around those appliances, and ask about specific cookware or utensils if a favourite recipe depends on them.

  • Groceries and everyday food

3. Your small coffee or tea favourite

If your morning depends on a particular brewing method, a compact brewer or tea infuser may earn its suitcase space. The kettle gives you a simple starting point in either apartment.

Cozy has an espresso machine; ask which coffee format it uses before buying supplies. Check Bright’s setup separately. Confirm equipment first, then decide what you need for that first cup on the balcony.

  • See Cozy’s kitchen and balcony

4. The cables that make your workspace work

Bring your device chargers, headphones and any laptop hub you normally use. Each apartment has a desk, chair, Wi-Fi and Ethernet connection; pack an Ethernet cable and the right device adapter if you intend to connect that way.

Ask about sockets and desk space before choosing travel adapters or bringing a monitor. Keep the small pieces together in one pouch so your first working morning begins with everything to hand.

  • Plan your work setup

5. A ready-to-go pool bag

Pack swimwear, your preferred goggles and a small bag for carrying damp things home. The residence has shared pools, making a short swim an easy addition to a day spent close to the apartment.

Ask whether towels suitable for pool and beach use are provided; bring your own compact towel if needed. Check the residence’s current pool hours and rules when you arrive.

6. Walking shoes and your workout basics

Choose comfortable shoes for outings and sandals you like wearing between the apartment and shared grounds. If you plan to use the shared gym, bring your usual training shoes, socks and workout clothes.

A familiar set is more useful than packing for every possible activity. Ask about current gym access and equipment before basing a training routine on a particular machine or class.

7. A light bag for sun and showers

Keep a hat, sunglasses, sunscreen, a refillable bottle and a compact umbrella together in a small day bag. A waterproof pouch helps keep your phone and essentials organised for pool days and outings.

Leave room for drinking water and a spare shirt. Packing this bag once makes it easier to step out for a waterfront wander or a planned beach afternoon without searching the apartment for small items.

  • Choose a beach outing

8. Your personal bathroom essentials

Bring the toiletries and grooming items you prefer, especially anything you would rather not spend your first evening shopping for. Keep a small arrival pouch accessible so you can freshen up while the rest of the suitcase stays closed.

Ask what toiletries and bathroom equipment are provided before leaving favourites behind. Confirm a hairdryer or other specific appliance directly if it matters to your routine.

9. A few familiar things for quiet evenings

A paperback, headphones or a small card game can make the living room and balcony feel like your own retreat. With children, choose a favourite bedtime book or compact toy that travels easily between the bedroom and living area.

Each home has a queen bed and sofa bed. Ask about cots, high chairs or other child equipment before packing around them; your booking team can confirm what is available for your stay.
