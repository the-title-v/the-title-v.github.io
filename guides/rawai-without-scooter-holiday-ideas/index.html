7 ways to enjoy Rawai without a scooter

A Rawai holiday can work without renting a scooter. Keep some days close to your apartment, book car rides for beach outings and leave room for a day by the pool. These seven ideas help you plan the week around what you actually want to do.

Updated 4 November 2026

Check dates See Bright & Cozy

1. Start with a direct airport arrival

Make the first journey straightforward by arranging a car from Phuket Airport to the residence. Share your flight details, luggage count and the exact Title V map pin with the driver; several developments have similar names. Agree the fare and pickup arrangements before travelling.

Our local agent handles the apartment handover. Send your expected arrival time to our booking team as well as to your driver. Check-in starts at 15:00; an earlier arrival needs a separate arrangement. Once you have unpacked, your holiday does not need to start with a vehicle-rental errand.

  • Compare airport arrival options
  • Save the residence location

2. Make a waterfront morning your first outing

Keep the first morning simple: breakfast in your own kitchen, then time beside Rawai's boat-lined waterfront. Come for the sea view, a coffee or a look at the longtail boats. Plan Nai Harn or Ya Nui as separate beach outings when you want time on the sand.

Check the walking route from the residence and choose crossings carefully. Keep outings short in the heat, carry water and take a car whenever the distance or walking conditions do not suit your group. A nearby destination does not mean every approach has a continuous pavement.

  • Get to know the neighbourhood

3. Combine food shopping with an easy meal out

A kitchen gives you the option of eating in between restaurant visits. Make a short shopping list for breakfasts, snacks and drinking water, then combine the grocery stop with lunch or dinner. Our food guide points you towards local starting places; check the shop's current opening hours before leaving.

Book a car back if you are carrying heavy bags. Stocking the fridge for a few days reduces repeated errands and leaves more time for the pool, reading or a slow breakfast on the balcony.

  • Food, cafés and groceries in Rawai

4. Give Nai Harn its own beach day

Book a car to Nai Harn and make the beach the main event. Its broad sandy bay and inland lake offer a different setting from Rawai's waterfront. Take what you need for the outing so you can settle in without returning to the apartment for forgotten items.

Arrange a return pickup or check ride availability before staying late. Grab offers car bookings in Thailand, but the vehicles, fare and wait depend on your exact request. Follow beach flags and lifeguard advice on arrival; a red flag means staying out of the water.

  • Nai Harn and Ya Nui beach guide
  • Grab car services

5. Book one car outing for an evening view

Choose Promthep Cape for an evening outing and agree the return journey before you leave. If you want to add another stop, discuss the route, waiting time and total fare with the driver. One planned outing is easier to manage than assuming a new car will be immediately available at every viewpoint.

Keep the plan flexible when rain or cloud changes the evening. The outing can finish with dinner in Rawai, or with something simple in your own apartment. You can enjoy the southern coast without committing to a full day on the road.

  • Plan an evening at Promthep Cape

6. Try a coastal bus outing when the timetable fits

Phuket Smart Bus connects Rawai with destinations along the west coast. Use the operator's current timetable and live tracking to choose a stop, check the return service and see whether the trip fits your day. Leave a generous margin for getting back.

Treat the bus as an option for a flexible outing. For a fixed appointment, an early flight or a journey with several bags, compare a direct car instead. Check the actual pickup point and fare before travelling; the bus does not collect guests from their apartment doors.

  • Smart Bus timetable and live tracking
  • More transport options in southern Phuket

7. Leave a whole day for the residence

Some of the easiest holiday days need no transport at all. At The Title V, you have shared pools and gardens, a gym and your own apartment to return to. Use the facilities within the residence's current hours and rules, then take lunch at home or sit on the balcony.

Bright and Cozy each have a kitchen, washing machine and separate bedroom, which makes a slower day comfortable. Our minimum stay is seven nights: choose your apartment, tell us your plans and check the dates for a week with room to unwind.
