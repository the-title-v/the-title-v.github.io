Your monthly budget · 4 min read

7 costs to check before booking a monthly condo in Rawai

A useful monthly budget shows what you will spend and what you need to set aside temporarily. Before reserving Bright or Cozy, make three columns: included in the stay estimate, payable separately, and refundable deposit. These seven checks will help you fill them in without counting a charge twice.

Updated 4 November 2026

Check dates See Bright & Cozy

1. Price every night of accommodation

Enter your arrival and departure dates, rather than multiplying a headline nightly rate by 30. Seasonal rates can change within one stay. Accommodation is included in the stay estimate; the minimum is seven nights, with 10% off accommodation for 7–27 nights and 20% off for 28 nights or longer.

Save the dated estimate and check the accommodation line before adding other costs. The refundable security deposit sits outside that figure. Payment and cancellation terms are confirmed in writing before you pay.

2. Count the guests, including children

The base rate includes two guests per apartment. Each additional child beyond that count costs ฿300 per night, included in the estimate when you enter the full group. Accommodation discounts do not reduce this charge; keep it in your spending column, separate from the refundable deposit.

For example, one additional child over 28 nights adds ฿8,400. Each home allows at most two adults and two children aged 14 or under. Guests aged 15 or older count as adults, so send the children’s ages before booking.

  • Plan a family stay

3. Check cleaning is counted once

Cleaning is ฿1,500 per apartment per stay, or ฿3,000 when booking both homes. It is already included in the calculated stay estimate and receives no weekly or monthly discount. Copy that line into your budget without adding it again to the total.

It is a stay charge, separate from the refundable deposit. If you would like cleaning during your month, ask what can be arranged and what it would cost; the stated fee is not a promise of regular housekeeping.

4. Leave room for metered electricity

Electricity costs ฿6 per kWh and is payable separately from the stay estimate. Both apartments have air conditioning in the bedroom and living area, so your own routine matters when planning a utility allowance.

Use the rate for scenarios: 100 kWh would cost ฿600. That is an arithmetic example, not a forecast of your consumption. Ask your local agent to explain the meter readings at handover, and keep electricity in the separate-payments column rather than treating the refundable deposit as a prepaid utility bill.

5. Add water as its own line

Water is metered at ฿55 per cubic metre and charged separately from the calculated stay total. For example, 5 m³ would cost ฿275; your bill depends on actual use. Having a washing machine is convenient, but water use still belongs in the budget.

Check how readings will be recorded and how the final utility payment will be settled. Keep water separate from accommodation, cleaning and the refundable security deposit so the end-of-stay calculation is easy to follow.

6. Set aside the refundable security deposit

The deposit is ฿10,000 per booking through 30 nights, or ฿20,000 from 31 nights. It is separate from the stay price and applies once per booking, including a booking for both apartments. Put it in the refundable column, while allowing for the funds you need to set aside.

A 28-, 29- or 30-night stay qualifies for the 20% accommodation discount with the ฿10,000 deposit. Confirm return arrangements and any deductions in writing before booking, especially if you are considering extending.

  • Deposit and booking terms

7. Match arrival and departure to the booking

Your booked stay uses check-in from 15:00 and check-out by 12:00, Phuket time. Early arrival or late departure arrangements depend on availability and may cost extra. Tell the team your flight times and ask for any charge separately before planning around extra apartment hours.

Add a confirmed charge to spending, separate from the refundable deposit, and ask how changes would be handled. Then review your three columns with the booking team: stay estimate, separate payments and refundable funds.
