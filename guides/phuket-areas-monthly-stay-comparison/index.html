7 Phuket areas compared for a monthly stay

For a month in Phuket, choose around your repeat trips: groceries, meals, work and time by the sea. Here is our take on seven areas. The exact apartment and the route to your everyday stops matter as much as the area name.

Updated 4 November 2026

The seven areas at a glance

Start with the routine you want, then check the particular apartment and its nearby services. These are suggestions for narrowing your shortlist.

1. Rawai: waterfront meals and an apartment routine

At Phuket’s southern end, Rawai puts longtail boats, seafood and waterfront meals into your everyday surroundings. Consider it if you want breakfast in your own kitchen, local errands and beach outings mixed with days at home. Treat the waterfront as your place for a stroll or dinner; plan a separate trip to Nai Harn or Ya Nui for a sandy beach day.

Our Bright and Cozy apartments at The Title V have kitchens, washers, desks and balconies, with shared pools at the residence. Send your full dates to check a monthly stay, and plan transport around the places you expect to visit repeatedly.

2. Nai Harn: make the southern bay your daily focus

Nai Harn sits north of Promthep Cape, with a sandy bay and a lake just inland. Shortlist it when returning to the same beach is a bigger part of your month than shopping or nightlife. Follow local beach flags and conditions when deciding whether to swim.

Check where the apartment actually sits relative to the bay. An address described as Nai Harn may still require a ride to the sand. Map your grocery stop and evening meals too, so the beach routine works on ordinary weekdays as well as outing days.

  • Nai Harn Beach · Phuket Province
  • Plan Nai Harn and Ya Nui outings

3. Kata: a beach holiday with visitor services nearby

Kata combines a southwest-coast beach setting with shops, accommodation and tour services. The name covers two distinct beaches, Kata Yai and Kata Noi, so decide which one you mean before comparing apartments. It is worth considering when you want regular beach time and options for organised outings during the month.

Ask for the actual walking route to your chosen beach entrance. A nearby map pin does not establish direct access across another property. Compare the kitchen, laundry and workspace too: holiday surroundings still need to support a month’s everyday tasks.

  • Kata’s two beaches · Phuket Province

4. Karon: a long stretch of sand to return to

North of Kata, Karon has a long sandy shore with accommodation, shops and visitor services in the surrounding area. Consider it if a beach walk is something you want to repeat often, while keeping Kata as another coastal outing.

Choose the part of Karon around your practical needs. Mark the apartment, beach entrance and food shops on the same map, then check the walking route and any road crossings. For a month, the daily journey with groceries matters more than simply having Karon in the address.

  • Karon Beach · Phuket Province (Thai)

5. Kamala: a beach-and-dinner routine on the west coast

Kamala lies north of Patong and south of Surin. Its beach area has restaurants, shops and accommodation, making it a useful candidate if you want to build a routine around one bay and a few familiar places to eat.

Compare a home near your preferred beach entrance with one farther inland. Check the evening route back and ask about the immediate surroundings, rather than assuming the whole area has one noise level. If Patong evenings are part of the plan, include those return journeys in your monthly transport budget.

  • Kamala Beach · Phuket Province

6. Bang Tao: choose between the shore and everyday shops

Bang Tao’s broad bay on the northwest coast includes a substantial resort area around Laguna Phuket. Boat Avenue, near Laguna’s entrance in Cherngtalay, offers another focus for cafés, restaurants and shops. The beachfront and the shopping area are different places to pin on your map.

Consider Bang Tao when you like having both beach time and dining choices in your plans, then choose which you want closest. Confirm the route from the particular apartment to each, and ask which facilities or transport services are actually included in that booking.

  • Bang Tao Beach · Phuket Province
  • Bang Tao and Boat Avenue · Laguna Phuket

7. Patong: shopping and evenings out around a busy bay

Patong brings a sandy west-coast bay together with shopping, restaurants and nightlife. Shortlist it if going out is central to your month and you want those choices in the same area as your accommodation.

For working days or early nights, ask where the bedroom faces and what surrounds the building. Proximity to entertainment can be an advantage or a drawback depending on your routine. Across all seven areas, confirm the complete dates, kitchen and work setup, utilities and booking terms for the exact home.

  • Patong Beach · Phuket Province
