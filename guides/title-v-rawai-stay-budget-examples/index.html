5 worked examples of a stay budget at The Title V

Your dates, length of stay and group determine the apartment estimate. These five examples use our published direct-stay rates and show accommodation, cleaning and any additional-child charges, with the refundable deposit kept separate. Electricity at ฿6/kWh and water at ฿55/m³ are extra. Food, transport and activities are outside these apartment budgets.

Updated 4 November 2026

Five examples at a glance

These are planning examples using rates reviewed on 4 November 2026, not offers of available dates. Check-in is included in the night count; check-out is not. The booking team confirms your dates, final price, payment schedule and cancellation terms in writing before payment.

Stays of 7–27 nights receive 10% off accommodation; 28 nights or more receive 20%. The discounts do not stack and do not reduce cleaning or additional-child charges.

1. A couple staying for a Christmas week

For two adults in Bright, arriving on 20 December and leaving on 27 December 2026, the seven occupied nights cross a rate change. The first three nights cost ฿3,300 each; the four nights from 23 December cost ฿4,500 each.

The weekly discount brings accommodation to ฿25,110. With cleaning, the stay estimate is ฿26,610. Set aside the refundable deposit separately, then allow for your actual metered utilities and everyday spending. A week with different dates can have a different total.

2. A family spending two weeks in February

This example puts two adults and two children aged 14 or under in Cozy from 1 to 15 February 2027. The base rate includes two guests; the two additional children add ฿300 each per night, or ฿8,400 over fourteen nights.

After the accommodation discount, the children's charges and cleaning bring the estimate to ฿51,480. The queen bedroom and living-room sofa bed are the sleeping spaces. Guests aged 15 or older count as adults, so include every child's age when requesting a stay.

3. A couple staying for all of February

From 1 February to 1 March 2027 is twenty-eight nights. For two adults in Bright, that qualifies for the 20% monthly accommodation discount, giving a stay estimate of ฿75,420 including cleaning. No additional-guest charge applies.

The refundable deposit remains ฿10,000 because this booking is no longer than thirty nights. A monthly discount is based on the number of nights, so you can also qualify without arriving on the first day of a calendar month. Use your exact dates to see the seasonal rates.

4. A couple making March their island month

Two adults staying in Cozy from 1 March to 1 April 2027 have thirty-one nights at the published March rate of ฿2,000. After the monthly discount and cleaning, their stay estimate is ฿51,100.

For bookings of 31 nights or more, the refundable deposit becomes ฿20,000 per booking. That changes the funds to set aside; it is not an extra accommodation charge. Confirm the deposit return arrangements in your written booking terms.

5. Two couples booking both apartments in May

For four adults, two in Bright and two in Cozy, 1–29 May 2027 is a twenty-eight-night booking for both adjacent apartments. Accommodation for both starts at ฿56,000 before the monthly discount. Cleaning is ฿3,000 for the pair, making the combined stay estimate ฿47,800.

The refundable deposit is ฿10,000 for this single booking covering both apartments. If the two couples divide the stay estimate equally, each couple's share is ฿23,900, before the deposit and utilities. Each home has its own entrance, kitchen and bathroom. Confirm both apartments are available together for your dates.

Turn an example into your own plan

Metered utilities depend on use. To illustrate the arithmetic only, 100 kWh of electricity plus 5 m³ of water would cost ฿875; those quantities are not a consumption forecast for any of these stays. Ask about readings and settlement at handover.

Enter your accommodation, dates and full group in the booking calendar, then send the request to our booking team. An enquiry does not reserve the dates. Keep the confirmed stay price, separate charges and refundable deposit as three clear parts of your plan.
