YOUR APARTMENT, WITH A LITTLE MORE AROUND IT

Come home
to the green.

The Title V is a low-rise condominium set back from Rawai’s waterfront. Garden paths connect the buildings and pools, making the shared grounds part of everyday life here.

Stay in your own one-bedroom apartment and use the residence’s shared facilities during their opening hours. The photographs on this page show the actual complex from our apartment listing galleries.

Shared pools wind through the residence, with loungers and planted courtyards around them. Make room for an easy afternoon close to your apartment.

Room to keep your routine

The shared fitness room gives you an option for a workout without an extra journey. The sauna and steam-room area offer another way to wind down.

The gym is modest in scale. Current equipment and opening hours may vary.

03

A change of workspace

Bring a laptop to the shared coworking area when you want a different setting. Your apartment also has its own desk and internet connection.

Shared seating is not a reserved private office.

Little explorers welcome

A children’s pool and a small outdoor playground make it easy to fit play into the day, with your own kitchen and bedroom nearby for the next break.

Children need adult supervision. No lifeguard or childcare service is promised.

GOOD TO KNOW BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

The practical
comforts.

Parking
The complex has shared parking. Let the booking team know if you will bring a car or motorbike so arrival and parking instructions can be confirmed.
Security
The residence is listed with 24-hour security and CCTV in common areas. Keep your entry details with you and follow the building’s visitor arrangements.
Elevator access
Both apartments are on the second floor and have elevator access. For a stroller, wheelchair or specific mobility need, ask the team to check the full access route and relevant dimensions.
A local welcome
A local agent meets and greets guests. Share your arrival plans with the booking team so your meeting point and check-in details are ready before you travel.
Shared facilities
Pools, gym, sauna, steam room and coworking areas are shared with other residents. Follow posted hours; maintenance and residence rules can affect access.
