Good to know

Sleeping arrangements

Up to 2 adults and 2 children aged 14 or under. A queen bed in the bedroom and a sofa bed in the living room. Guests aged 15 or older count as adults. Include children’s ages in your request.

A local welcome

A local agent welcomes you. Second-floor apartment with elevator access.

Arrival and departure

Check-in from 3 pm; check-out by noon. Early or late arrangements may be available at extra cost.

Cleaning and additional guests

The calculator includes ฿1,500 cleaning per stay. The base rate covers 2 guests; each additional child beyond that count costs ฿300 per night.

Refundable deposit and utilities

Refundable deposit: ฿10,000 per booking up to 30 nights, or ฿20,000 for longer stays. Metered electricity is ฿6/kWh and water ฿55/m³, charged separately from the stay estimate.

House rules

No pets, no smoking. Keep noise low, respect neighbours and follow all condominium rules.

Around the residence

Pools, gym, sauna, and coworking are shared facilities. Opening hours and access follow the residence’s rules.
