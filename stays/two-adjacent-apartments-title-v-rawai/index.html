Good to know

Two independent homes

These are adjacent apartments with separate entrances. There is no internal connecting door.

Plan the sleeping arrangement

Up to 4 adults and 4 children aged 14 or under across both apartments. Each apartment has a queen bed and sofa bed, with a maximum of 2 adults and 2 children. Include children’s ages in your enquiry.

Both calendars matter

The pair can only be reserved when both apartments are available for your full stay. Send one inquiry for the entire group.

Cleaning and additional guests

The calculator includes ฿3,000 cleaning for both apartments. The base rate covers 4 guests; each additional child beyond that count costs ฿300 per night.

A local welcome

A local agent welcomes you. Second-floor apartment with elevator access.

Arrival and departure

Check-in from 3 pm; check-out by noon. Early or late arrangements may be available at extra cost.

Refundable deposit and utilities

Refundable deposit: ฿10,000 per booking up to 30 nights, or ฿20,000 for longer stays. Metered electricity is ฿6/kWh and water ฿55/m³, charged separately from the stay estimate.

House rules

No pets, no smoking. Keep noise low, respect neighbours and follow all condominium rules.

Around the residence

Pools, gym, sauna, and coworking are shared facilities. Opening hours and access follow the residence’s rules.
