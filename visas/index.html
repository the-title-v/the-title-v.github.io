Make room for a longer stay.

Choose a visa that fits your plans. These routes need an application and approval before travel.

UP TO 60 DAYS PER ENTRY

Tourist Visa (TR)

A holiday with more time to settle in. Apply in advance through Thai e-Visa from outside Thailand.

How to prepare

Use your passport and current location to find the responsible embassy. Prepare your passport, photo, travel and accommodation plans, and financial evidence, following its checklist.

Submit the form, upload the documents, pay the visa fee and wait for approval. Fees and processing times depend on the embassy.

For repeated visits, check the multiple-entry Tourist Visa with your embassy. Its validity and each permitted stay are separate limits.

UP TO 180 DAYS PER ENTRY

Destination Thailand Visa (DTV)

For qualifying remote workers or freelancers, participants in activities such as Muay Thai, Thai cooking or medical treatment, and eligible family members.

Citizenship alone does not establish eligibility.

Who it suits & what you need
  • Evidence of qualifying overseas work or a professional portfolio, confirmed activity or treatment, or your relationship to a DTV holder. The family route covers a spouse or children under 20.
  • Financial evidence of at least ฿500,000, with the documents and statement history your embassy requests.
  • Proof of permanent residence and a certificate of criminal record clearance, required for new applications from 31 August 2026. Confirm the exact documents with your mission.

Apply outside Thailand in a country where you are a citizen or permanent resident. Follow that embassy’s checklist and submit through Thai e-Visa.

The visa has five-year validity and multiple entries. Each entry allows up to 180 days; a further extension of up to 180 days per entry may be requested from Thai Immigration.

ONE LAST THING BEFORE YOU FLY

Your arrival form.
It’s free.

Non-Thai travellers entering Thailand need the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC), including when travelling with a visa or visa exemption.

Complete it on the official Immigration website within the three days before arrival. Have your passport, journey and Thailand accommodation details ready. The arrival form does not replace a visa.
