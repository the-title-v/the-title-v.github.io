Families and extra guests

Each apartment accommodates up to 2 adults and 2 children aged 14 or under, with a queen bed and sofa bed. Guests aged 15 or older count as adults. Each additional child beyond the 2 included guests costs ฿300 per night.

For both apartments, the base rate includes 4 guests, with a maximum of 4 adults and 4 children across the pair. The same per-apartment capacity applies.

A seven-night example

One apartment in green season with 2 adults: ฿7,000 accommodation − ฿700 weekly discount + ฿1,500 cleaning = ฿7,800 stay total. The refundable deposit is ฿10,000 separately; water and electricity are charged by use.

With 2 adults and 2 children aged 14 or under, add 2 × ฿300 × 7 nights = ฿4,200. The stay total becomes ฿12,000, plus the same refundable deposit and metered utilities.

Across seasons or across two apartments

A stay spanning two seasons uses the correct rate for each night before the length-of-stay discount. Booking both apartments doubles the base accommodation and cleaning amounts; both calendars must be available for the full stay.

A local welcome

A local agent meets and greets you. Check-in is from 3:00 pm and check-out is by 12:00 noon, Phuket time. Early check-in and late check-out can be negotiated, subject to availability, and may cost extra.

No pets, no smoking, keep noise low and follow the condominium rules. See the full booking details.

An estimate does not reserve dates. Our booking team confirms availability, the final offer, payment and cancellation terms in writing before you book.
