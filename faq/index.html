THE LITTLE THINGS, EXPLAINED

Good to know.

A few useful answers before you make yourself at home.

How do I book?

Choose your apartment and dates, then send the prepared WhatsApp enquiry. Our team confirms the price, availability and payment details with you.

What is the minimum stay?

Seven nights. Stay 7–27 nights for 10% off accommodation, or 28 nights or more for 20% off. The calculator applies the discount automatically.

Who meets us on arrival?

Our local agent welcomes you, hands over the keys and shows you around. Send us your arrival time beforehand.

How many people can stay?

Each apartment sleeps 2 adults and 2 children aged 14 or under, in a queen bed and sofa bed. Book both apartments for up to 4 adults and 4 children. Guests aged 15+ count as adults.

Are children included in the price?

Two guests are included per apartment. Each additional child costs ฿300 per night.

What else do I pay?

Cleaning is ฿1,500 per apartment, included in the estimate. Electricity (฿6/kWh) and water (฿55/m³) are charged by use. A refundable deposit is paid separately.

How much is the deposit?

฿10,000 per booking for up to 30 nights; ฿20,000 for longer stays. The same deposit applies when booking both apartments. We agree on payment and return details before you book.

When can we check in and out?

Check-in is from 3 pm; check-out is by noon, Phuket time. Ask about earlier arrival or later departure — availability and extra charges may apply.

What are the house rules?

No pets or smoking. Keep noise low, respect your neighbours and follow the residence’s rules.

Is the calendar up to date?

It uses updates from Airbnb, with the last successful update shown beside it. Our team confirms your dates before booking.

Do the two apartments connect?

They are next door, each with its own entrance, kitchen and bathroom. There is no internal connecting door.

Can we swim at Rawai Beach?

Rawai is best for waterfront walks, seafood and island boats. Head to Nai Harn or Ya Nui for a beach day, and follow the swimming flags.

How fast is the Wi-Fi?

Both apartments have 100 Mbps internet, a desk and Ethernet. The original Cozy speed test shows 97.7 Mbps download. Ask for a fresh test if you need it for work.
