THE LITTLE THINGS, EXPLAINED

Good to know.

A few useful answers before you make yourself at home.

How do I book directly?

Choose an apartment, dates and your group to see a stay estimate. Open the prepared WhatsApp request. Our booking team confirms availability, the final price, payment arrangements and cancellation terms in writing before you book.

Who welcomes us on arrival?

A local agent meets and greets you, handles the apartment handover and explains the essentials. Share your arrival time in advance so the welcome can be arranged.

How many adults and children can stay?

Each apartment accommodates up to 2 adults and 2 children aged 14 or under, with a queen bed and a sofa bed. Guests aged 15 or older count as adults. The two-apartment option accommodates up to 4 adults and 4 children, respecting the same limit in each apartment. Please include children’s ages in your request.

How are extra guests priced?

The base rate includes 2 guests per apartment, or 4 when booking both. Each additional child beyond that included count is ฿300 per night. The calculator adds this separately; accommodation discounts do not reduce extra-guest or cleaning fees.

Are weekly and monthly discounts included?

The calculator applies 10% off the accommodation subtotal for 7–27 nights, or 20% for 28 nights or more. Discounts do not stack. Each night uses its seasonal rate. Cleaning and extra-guest fees are separate.

What does the stay estimate include?

It includes accommodation after any length-of-stay discount, extra-guest charges and a one-time cleaning fee of ฿1,500 per apartment (฿3,000 for both). The refundable deposit and metered water and electricity are shown separately.

How much is the refundable security deposit?

The refundable deposit is ฿10,000 per booking for stays of up to 30 nights and ฿20,000 for 31 nights or longer, including when booking both apartments together. It is separate from the stay price. Return arrangements and any documented deductions are agreed in writing before booking.

How are electricity and water charged?

Electricity is charged by meter at ฿6 per kWh and water at ฿55 per cubic metre (m³). These usage-based charges are additional to the calculator’s stay estimate. Meter readings and settlement arrangements are explained at handover.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 3:00 pm and check-out is by 12:00 noon, Phuket time. Early check-in or late check-out can be negotiated, subject to availability, and may require an additional payment. Arrange this before arrival or departure.

What are the house rules?

No pets and no smoking. Keep noise low, respect neighbours and follow all condominium rules, including posted rules for the pools, gym and other shared facilities. The local agent explains access and practical details at arrival.

Are the calendars connected to Airbnb?

Yes. Availability uses snapshots of the apartments’ Airbnb calendars, with the last update shown beside the calendar. Updates can take time to propagate, so an open date is an indication rather than a guaranteed reservation. Our booking team confirms every stay.

Can I book both apartments together?

Yes. Choose “Two Adjacent 1BR Apartments in Title V Rawai”. These are two separate neighbouring apartments with their own entrances, bedrooms, bathrooms and kitchens. There is no advertised internal connecting door. Both calendars must be available.

Is Rawai Beach a swimming beach?

Rawai is known for its waterfront, fishing boats, seafood and departures to nearby islands. Nai Harn and Ya Nui are options for a beach day; always follow local beach flags and conditions. See the location page and local guides for practical suggestions.

Can I work from the apartments?

Both apartments include Wi-Fi and a dedicated workspace. The residence also has shared coworking space. Tell our booking team about specific connectivity or equipment requirements before booking.
