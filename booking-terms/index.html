CLEAR BEFORE YOU CONFIRM

The details of your stay.

See the price components and practical arrangements here, then agree on your booking in writing with our team.

From a request to a reservation

Select an apartment, dates and your group, review the estimate, then open the prepared WhatsApp message. Sending an enquiry does not hold dates or create a confirmed reservation.

A reservation is confirmed when you and our booking team have agreed on availability, the final total, payment arrangements and all applicable terms in writing.

Adults, children and extra guests

Each apartment accommodates up to 2 adults and 2 children aged 14 or under. Guests aged 15 or older count as adults. Booking both apartments allows up to 4 adults and 4 children, with the same limit respected in each apartment. Each has one queen bed and one sofa bed.

The base rate includes 2 guests per apartment (4 for both). Additional children beyond the included count cost ฿300 each per night. Include children’s ages so the sleeping arrangement can be confirmed.

Nightly rates, discounts and cleaning

The calculator uses a seasonal rate for every occupied night and supports stays of 2–180 nights. Accommodation receives a 10% discount for 7–27 nights or 20% for 28 nights or more; the discounts do not stack.

A one-time cleaning fee of ฿1,500 per apartment is included in the stay estimate, or ฿3,000 for both apartments. Extra-guest charges and cleaning are not discounted. Additional cleaning during a stay can be discussed separately.

Refundable security deposit

The refundable security deposit is ฿10,000 per booking for stays up to 30 nights and ฿20,000 for stays of 31 nights or more. This applies to the single-apartment and two-apartment options.

The deposit is separate from the stay price. Payment, return arrangements and any documented deductions are agreed in writing before booking. It is not a cleaning fee or accommodation charge.

Water and electricity

Water is metered at ฿55 per cubic metre (m³), and electricity at ฿6 per kilowatt-hour (kWh). Usage charges are additional to the calculated stay estimate.

Our local agent explains meter readings and settlement arrangements at handover. Actual charges depend on your consumption.

Arrival and departure

Our local agent meets and greets you and handles the apartment handover. Please provide your arrival time in advance. Check-in is from 3:00 pm and check-out is by 12:00 noon, Phuket time.

Early check-in and late check-out can be negotiated, subject to availability, and may require an additional payment. Please arrange any change with the team ahead of time.

House rules

No pets. No smoking. Keep noise low and be considerate of neighbours, especially at night.

Follow all condominium rules and posted instructions for the pools, gym, sauna, parking and other shared spaces. The local agent will explain the practical details at arrival.

Calendar information

Availability uses imported Airbnb calendar data, and updates can be delayed. An unblocked date indicates possible availability; our booking team confirms every reservation. The two-apartment option checks both calendars.

If availability cannot be verified, the calculator makes that clear. You can still ask the booking team to check your preferred dates.

Payment, cancellation and changes

Payment methods, payment schedule, cancellation conditions, refunds and changes are agreed in writing before booking. The website does not collect payments or card details.

Review the complete terms before paying. A stay estimate is not a confirmed reservation or a promise of a particular cancellation refund.
