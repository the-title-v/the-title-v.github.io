A SIMPLE WEBSITE. CLEAR INFORMATION.

Your privacy, simply.

Explore the apartments and prepare an enquiry. You decide when to open WhatsApp and send your message.

Your selections

The calculator processes your apartment, dates and adult/child counts in your browser. Links can include these selections in their URL. The website does not ask you to register or enter payment details.

Your browser loads the selected apartment’s calendar update from a static file. It contains blocked dates and the last successful update time, never guest names, reservation titles or private calendar links.

WhatsApp, maps and other services

Opening a booking request includes your apartment, dates, group and estimate in a WhatsApp link. You choose whether to send it; WhatsApp handles that conversation under its own privacy terms.

The embedded location map and links to Google Maps use Google’s services. Loading the map may share ordinary connection information with the map provider. Airbnb and other source links use their own privacy policies.

Storage and technical data

This site has no advertising trackers, analytics scripts, registration or cookie-based booking profile. Apartment photographs and fonts are hosted with the website.

The hosting service may process normal technical request logs. Calendar data is imported before publication and may become outdated. Updates older than one hour are shown as unverified. Private calendar data is not published.

Questions about your enquiry

Contact our booking team through the same WhatsApp conversation for questions about information you share directly. This website does not hold a separate customer account or payment record.
