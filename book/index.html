How direct booking works

  1. Choose your home

    Select Bright, Cozy or both adjacent apartments at The Title V in Rawai. Enter your dates, adult count and children aged 14 or under. Stays start at seven nights.

  2. Review your estimate

    The stay estimate includes accommodation, the applicable weekly or monthly discount, cleaning and any extra-child charges. Metered electricity, water and the refundable security deposit are separate.

  3. Send your enquiry

    Review the prepared WhatsApp message and send it when you are ready. Our team confirms your dates, final price, payment schedule and cancellation terms in writing before you pay.

An enquiry does not hold dates or create a confirmed reservation. Check the calendar’s last successful update; our team confirms availability for your full stay, including both homes when you request the pair.
